Metronome brings traffic, transit, weather and event data together into one pulse score for every London borough.

## One city, many signals

The interesting question is how to bring those different signals into a single view without hiding what makes each borough distinct. A future post could explain how the score is calculated, how the data is refreshed, and what happens when an external API is unavailable.
