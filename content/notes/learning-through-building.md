Some ideas are easier to understand when they become part of a real project. This could be a place to write about one concept I have worked through, with a small example and the questions it raised.

## Start with one question

A learning note does not have to cover an entire topic. It can begin with something specific: how a piece of state stays consistent, why a query is slow, or what a failing test helped reveal.

> A small, clear explanation can be a useful thing to share.
